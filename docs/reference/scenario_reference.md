# Scenario reference — society, peoples, and daily life

Public/portfolio-safe reference for the TTRPG scenario side of this project. Distilled from the
Tappa 7 brainstorm and the original "Ambientação" draft, with a lot revised since that draft was
written — treat this as the current state, not the draft. Some deeper lore is deliberately withheld
here and kept in a separate, non-public file by design.

## 1. Círculos — society and governance

Human settlements, called Círculos, are decentralized and self-governed. Most decisions are made
in assemblies or councils, renewed regularly. Social roles are flexible, with encouragement toward
versatility, study, and individual expression. There are no rigid hierarchies, prisons, or violent
punishment. Ethical principles are known as "o Compasso" (rules not yet fully defined). Conflict
is handled through listening and mediation; in extreme cases, through exile — not lethal, but a
hard fate, marked by separation from community life and visible marks so other Círculos recognize
the person's status. Over time, some exiled people formed their own groups (see Povo Livre,
below).

## 2. Settlement tiers

Three human settlement tiers, consistent with the population bands already locked in Tappa 6
(4 large Círculos at 20-40k, 5 medium at 2k, 8 small at 625):

- **Pequenos** — a few hundred people, close-knit, craft/sustainability/nature-focused.
- **Médios** — 1,000-10,000, organized councils specialized by area (education, agriculture,
  science).
- **Grandes** — tens of thousands, defined hierarchies, trade networks, broader political
  influence — where cultural tension and social innovation manifest most intensely.

## 3. Daily life and culture

Life is marked by natural cycles, celebration, learning, and creation. Relationship and family
configurations are diverse and widely accepted; gender and sexual orientation are understood
fluidly, with no social hierarchy tied to either. Differences are recognized, not ranked — though
subtle tensions exist: ideological and generational divergence, and the open question of how each
Círculo relates to its own history.

**Funerary rite**: when someone dies, anyone who knew them may contribute to a collective memorial
book about their life — not necessarily flattering, reflecting real impressions. Book length
scales with the person's impact on the community. Books are stored in libraries called
**Arquigrados**. The body is cremated or composted.

## 5. Povo Livre

Nomadic/semi-nomadic people, forestry-foraging economy (per Tappa 6). Widely believed within Círculo
society to be descendants of people exiled over the generations — genuine encounters between the two
societies are limited, and most of what Círculo people believe about Povo Livre life is secondhand or
rumor rather than firsthand knowledge.

That unfamiliarity feeds a persistent, generalized distrust in many Círculos: a common belief that
Povo Livre shelter, or are at least complicit with, people who have committed serious harm within
Círculo society. Whether or how that belief connects to reality isn't something Círculo society has
any confirmed way of knowing — the gap in knowledge deepens the divide it's built on, rather than
resolving it.

Their forest expertise has a practical, protective payoff: Wet Forest's seasonal toxic bloom (an
extremely beautiful but dangerous flowering, see `07_tappa7_regional_scenario.md`) is hazardous to
outsiders but well understood by Povo Livre, who know its timing and locations and how to work
around it — one concrete way their knowledge reads as valuable rather than merely different from
Círculo life.

## 6. Povo Silencioso

A reptilian, non-human species native to this world, inhabiting the NE archipelago, known to
outsiders as "Povo Silencioso" for their apparent lack of communication. Little else is confirmed
publicly — genuine encounters are rare, given their isolation, and most of what circulates is
secondhand description rather than verified fact.

## 7. Domesticated animals

Working and companion species kept across Círculo and Povo Livre society, organized here by biome
or ecotype the same way the wild fauna sections below are.

- **Yak** — alpine/Spine niche riding and pack animal. Real working precedent: yaks are genuine 
  pack/draft animals in Tibet.
- **Reindeer** — riding and pack animal, diverged into a lighter Círculo Grassland ecotype and a
  smaller, more maneuverable Povo Livre forest ecotype. Real reindeer already show this kind of
  natural ecotype diversity (tundra/mountain/woodland) even without deliberate breeding, and real
  domesticated-breed divergence can occur within just a few centuries of selective breeding,
  comfortably supporting a split like this. Real working precedent: reindeer are used for both
  riding and sledding among Arctic peoples. (Deer were considered and ruled out: real deer have leg
  structure built for running/leaping rather than load-bearing, and an undomesticated, skittish
  temperament — reindeer are the real, working exception.) 
- **Sheep** — wool, meat, and milk; the in-world source of the wool behind. Diverged into a lowland 
  Grassland ecotype (the majority, general-purpose) and a smaller, hardier mountain ecotype for 
  alpine communities — a real, well-documented pattern in actual sheep breeding (lowland vs. 
  hill/mountain breeds), not an invented split.
- **Dogs** — herding and companionship, not tied to a single biome. Diverged into a Círculo herding
  type, working closely with sheep, and a Povo Livre companion/tracking type suited to forest
  foraging, more independent in temperament since it isn't managing a flock — mirrors the real,
  well-attested split between herding and hunting/tracking dog breeds.
- **Chickens** — eggs and meat, also not tied to a single biome. Diverged furthest of the group: a
  common Círculo dooryard type; a cold-hardy alpine type for Terrapedra communities (real
  poultry-breeding fact — smaller combs and heavier feathering evolved specifically against
  frostbite at altitude); and a more free-ranging Povo Livre landrace, suited to foraging rather
  than a fixed coop.

Beyond the Círculos and Povo Livre settlements, small alpine outposts also exist along the Spine,
built for resource work and highland study rather than general habitation — home ground for yak in
particular, since the Círculos themselves sit outside alpine terrain entirely. Some outposts have
been abandoned over time. Yak, reindeer, and sheep can all go feral at an abandoned outpost and
persist without human management (real precedent: feral cattle, horses, goats, and sheep are all
well-documented — Camargue horses, Chillingham cattle); dogs and chickens don't, lacking the same
real-world precedent for independent survival. Reindeer have no dedicated feral ecotype the way yak
and sheep do — their presence at an abandoned site is opportunistic rather than a distinct bred
variant.

## 8. Coastal and marine fauna

Native (island-resident) and migratory (seasonal-visitor) species, built niche by niche starting
at the coast and working inland toward the still-open tundra apex predator (see
`07_tappa7_regional_scenario.md`). Migratory visitors species are explicitly not counted as island fauna.

**Native residents**

- **Shadowless Sardine** — small (~15-22 cm) schooling coastal food fish, the base of the coastal
  fishing economy, population tied to the same wind-driven upwelling already established for this
  coastline (real analog: the Peruvian anchoveta/Humboldt Current fishery, the largest single
  fishery on Earth by tonnage, sustained by the same wind-driven mechanism). Metallic red flanks
  and back — structural color from guanine platelet stacks, the same mechanism behind ordinary fish
  "silvering," here tuned to a different reflected wavelength. Ventral counter-illumination
  photophores (a real trait borrowed from deep/twilight-zone lanternfish and hatchetfish, applied
  unusually to a coastal surface schooler) stay pale blue-green rather than red: seawater filters
  out red wavelengths within a few meters of depth, so blue-green is the only band that actually
  functions as camouflage against downwelling light at any depth that matters.
- **Moonfur** — coastal pinniped, ~2.8-3.2 m / 350-500 kg (Weddell-seal size class), with real
  Weddell-seal-derived enlarged eyes for low-light hunting. True phosphorescent afterglow fur —
  charges under sustained surface light exposure, fades slowly in true darkness. Because a brief
  hunting pass near a Shadowless Sardine school doesn't expose the coat  long enough to charge it
  meaningfully, but sustained surface time does, Moonfur descends and waits in the deep before
  starting a hunt — a real behavioral consequence of the mechanism, not just a color trait.

  Home range is the island's southwest and west coast, opposite Povo Silencioso's NE archipelago,
  so the two don't compete over the same Shadowless Sardine stock. That same southwest/west stretch
  is also where Sea Terror (below) is sometimes found when it arrives — the two ranges genuinely
  overlap, which is exactly what forces the response below rather than it being an arbitrary
  pairing. When Sea Terror moves in, Moonfur relocates temporarily — southeast or north/northwest —
  rather than contest territory or risk becoming prey, returning once Sea Terror moves on. Their
  movement away from the southwest is a recognized folk warning sign: when Moonfur are seen leaving,
  it means danger on the water.

**Migratory visitors** (seasonal, not resident island fauna)

| species | real anchor | appearance | duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green Mackerel | Atlantic mackerel — green-blue iridescent, dark banding, dense schooling, prized food fish | Late spring-summer, timed to the upwelling productivity peak | Weeks to ~2 months, tracks bloom strength |
| Sea Terror | Saltwater crocodile (*Crocodylus porosus*), ~6 m — at the real documented maximum for the species (Lolong, verified 6.17 m) | Irregular, not annual — opportunistic long-distance dispersal, arriving after Green Mackerel when it comes at all | A few weeks, unpredictable |
| Bronzeshell | Leatherback turtle (*Dermochelys coriacea*), slimmer build, longer/thinner flippers than the real species already has, bronze/ochre-yellow coloring | Nesting season, early summer, south-island beaches | Females ashore briefly to nest; offshore presence longer |
| Stained Penguin | Penguin foraging migration (real: Magellanic penguin); near-white body, dark head/neck spotting that is molt-encoded — feather pigment is fixed at growth, so the spots record how much time the bird spent off-ice during its last molt cycle rather than changing live. More common on the south island. | Late winter-early spring | Days to a few weeks per visit, can recur |
| Skydrifter | Long-distance flyway transit (real: migrants routinely overfly stopover sites without landing) — medium size, colorful, in transit between two other landmasses | Spring and autumn passage windows | Overflight mostly instantaneous; rare landings, hours to a day |
| SummerVisitant (Wetducks) | Steamer duck (*Tachyeres*) — weak/flightless-leaning flight, exceptional swimming and diving instead | Seasonal, opposite the real Visitantes' winter-solstice timing | Present on interior lakes for part of the year; migration origin/destination unconfirmed — see §14 |

A persistent, unconfirmed fishermen's rumor — something moving beneath Shadowless Sardine schools
that scatter without visible cause, never actually sighted — is folded into "the hunter of the deep
seas" (§19, Legends and Rumors) rather than built out as its own species.

## 9. Grassland fauna

Grassland is the island's dominant biome (13 of the 17 placed Círculos sit within it), so its
single large-grazer niche carries outsized ecological weight — reasoned and built as a keystone
species, not just "a big herbivore."

- **Grassmothers** — bison-anchored bovid (real citation: American bison, *Bison bison*, a genuine
  ecosystem engineer whose grazing and wallowing shape grassland plant diversity), matriarchal and
  female-dominant. Real anchor for the social structure: the spotted hyena (*Crocuta crocuta*), the
  best-documented real mammal case of females both larger and socially dominant over males — a
  different, rarer mechanism than elephant-style matriarchy, where the lead female is senior but
  not physically larger. Females ~800-1000 kg, ~2.1-2.2 m at the shoulder; males 18% lighter,
  ~650-800 kg, ~1.9-2.0 m — a deliberately real, moderate size gap rather than an exaggerated one,
  consistent with the actual hyena precedent.

  Species-wide base coat and skin run pale — a functional adaptation, not just a way to distinguish
  them from yak (though it does that too): yak's dark coat is a real cold-climate adaptation for
  solar heat absorption at altitude, while Grassmothers live across open, sun-exposed lowland
  range, where real desert/open-plains ungulates like the Arabian oryx instead run pale coats
  specifically to reflect solar heat. Males additionally grow a wool-textured mane/ruff in the
  reddish-brown-to-black-and-yellow range, sometimes with pale stripes or patches (real citation:
  bongo, kudu, and nyala antelope carry warm-toned coats with disruptive pale striping, which can
  double as individual recognition within a herd) — functioning as a visual and scent-based (wool
  retains scent-marking secretions better than smooth guard hair, a real mechanism in ungulate
  chemical communication) display toward the larger, choosing females, consistent with general
  sexual-selection logic even though the specific sex-role pairing is a deliberate reversal of the
  bovid norm.

  Long-range herd coordination is via real infrasonic communication (a genuine cross-class
  recombination — infrasound over kilometers is otherwise essentially unique to elephants and
  cetaceans among real animals, never documented in bovids), functionally suited to coordinating
  herds dispersed across the island's unusually large Grassland range.

  **Regional ecotype**: in the windiest parts of the Grassland — the same exposed terrain shaped by
  the island's dominant, consistent wind bearing — Grassmothers grow a heavy fringe of fur over the
  eyes, real citation: Highland cattle's forelock, which shields the eyes from wind, rain, and
  insects in exposed weather. Elsewhere on the island, head fur stays short and standard. This is a
  coat-length ecotype variation, not a subspecies split — the same real pattern already used for
  Terrapedra's cold-hardy chickens and mountain-ecotype sheep elsewhere in this document.

## 10. Forest and Woodland-Shrubland fauna — mid-size browsers

Two species, one per biome, each a real herbivore template combined with one cross-lineage
recombination, following the same "real trait from elsewhere on Earth, in a combination Earth
never produced" method used throughout.

- **Blacknose** — Temperate Forest resident, tapir-anchored (~150-300 kg): prehensile browsing
  snout, real citation for ecological role — real tapirs are documented "megagardeners of the
  forest," their selective browsing and long-distance seed dispersal genuinely shaping forest tree
  composition, a keystone role distinct from Grassmothers' grazing-pressure role. Independently
  mobile eyes (real chameleon trait, absent from any mammal) give near-360° passive vigilance while
  the head stays down foraging in undergrowth — a genuine cross-class recombination, functionally
  suited to an animal that spends long stretches head-down and vulnerable. Name is physical,
  following real tapir species' commonly dark/black snout and forequarters (e.g. Malayan tapir).

- **Tailstand** — Woodland-Shrubland resident, gerenuk-anchored (~50-80 kg, scaled up from the
  real species' 30-52 kg to read clearly as mid-size rather than crowding the small-mammals niche
  still ahead): long neck, real gerenuk bipedal browsing behavior (rearing onto hind legs to reach
  foliage other browsers can't). Real gerenuk can only hold this stance briefly, unsupported;
  grafting a real kangaroo-style thick balancing tail — a genuine tripod support absent from any
  real bovid — lets Tailstand sustain the bipedal stance far longer than the real animal manages.
  Marsupial locomotor mechanism transplanted onto a bovid body plan, extending an existing real
  behavior into a combination Earth never produced. Name is behavioral, following the same
  convention as Skydrifter and Grassmothers.

## 11. Reptiles and amphibians

Three species (originally scoped at two; a third was added deliberately once the design work
suggested it). Each pairs a real base template with one cross-lineage recombination, and this
niche also introduces a second layer: real aposematic (warning) coloration, used deliberately
rather than decoratively.

- **Mudlizard** — riverbank/wetland resident, Nile-monitor-anchored (~1.2-1.8 m). Kept moderate in
  scale on purpose, so it doesn't compete with the dedicated mesopredator niche still ahead. Real
  platypus electroreception (otherwise unique to monotremes among real animals) grafted onto
  sensitive snout/jowl skin, for hunting prey hidden in mud or murky water where sight doesn't
  help — the same functional logic real platypuses already use, just transplanted into a reptile.
  Beyond the mainland and the SW island, Mudlizard is also the likeliest resident on the larger of
  the many small islets scattered off the coast — real monitor lizards are capable overwater
  colonizers. Birds are the default across that same scattered ground otherwise, as they would be
  for any remote small island.

- **Flashfrog** — Wet Forest resident, poison-dart-frog-anchored (a few centimeters; extreme real
  toxicity doesn't require size). Toxin is dietarily sequestered, not self-synthesized — a real,
  documented mechanism in real poison frogs — and here it's sequestered specifically from insects
  that feed on Wet Forest's toxic bloom, a direct ecological consequence of lore already
  established rather than an invented separate hazard. Real cephalopod chromatophores (neurally
  controlled, sub-second color change — categorically faster than any real vertebrate's pigment
  system, including slow hormonal chameleon color-shifting) grafted onto its skin for a rapid
  aposematic flash-display.

- **Clicksnake** — Grassland resident, prairie-rattlesnake-anchored (~1.2-1.5 m), venomous — a
  deliberate everyday-scale danger for the island's main Círculo biome, distinct from the
  mythic-tier threats (§19, Legends and Rumors) and the migratory-tier ones (Sea Terror). Real
  infrared pit-organ heat sensing carries over unmodified as baseline biology. Real
  echolocation — otherwise unique to bats and toothed whales among real animals — is grafted on,
  but deliberately pitched in the human-audible range rather than ultrasonic; the honest real
  citation for audible-frequency echolocation is oilbirds and cave swiftlets, not bats, and it's
  correspondingly coarser-resolution: enough to sense a close, slow target through tall grass,
  not fine enough to track something fast and small the way bat sonar does. Clicksnake has no
  separate rattle organ — the same click mechanism does both jobs, running as soft ambient clicks
  while hunting and escalating to loud, fast clicking paired with an aggressive raised posture when
  threatened, a single-mechanism analog of how real rattlesnakes already scale rattle intensity
  with perceived threat. The clicking is incidentally audible to people nearby, functioning as an
  unintentional warning — the same indirect-evidence pattern as Moonfur's territorial shift and the
  sky-watcher's feathers. Red eyes and black-and-yellow striping serve double duty: real
  aposematic warning coloration (the same black/yellow/red palette real coral snakes and their
  mimics use) and a deliberate, distant echo of male Grassmothers' coloring, since the two species
  share the same Grassland habitat — real unrelated species converging on similar coloration under
  shared habitat pressure is a documented phenomenon, not a coincidence.

## 12. Small mammals

Three species (expanded from the originally scoped two). Real base templates, cross-lineage
recombination, and — new to this niche — a case of habitat-dependent trait effectiveness
deliberately built into the design rather than assumed.

- **Scattermouse** — Woodland-Shrubland resident, jerboa-anchored (real bipedal leaping
  locomotion). Lives in large mobile groups (real citation: meerkat mobs of 20-50 with
  cooperative vigilance — a genuine cross-family recombination, since real jerboas are solitary
  or loosely paired, not mob-forming). When a predator strikes, the group performs a coordinated
  multidirectional scatter — real citation: the "confusion effect"/"flash expansion," a
  documented anti-predator strategy in various real prey species — overwhelming the predator's
  ability to track a single target. Population resilience despite predation losses is explained
  by genuine r-selected life history (real r/K selection theory: high fecundity, fast
  generational turnover offsetting losses).

- **Quillhog** — wide-ranging, not confined to a single biome, hedgehog-anchored. Baseline real
  behavior: self-anointing, where real hedgehogs chew mildly toxic substances into a foamy saliva
  and spread it across their spines — a real, if still not fully explained, defense behavior. Two
  recombinations: barbed quill microstructure (real porcupine trait, genuinely different from a
  hedgehog's smooth spines, making them far harder to remove once embedded) and a toxin source
  that varies by region, consistent with real hedgehogs' opportunistic use of whatever's locally
  available — in Wet Forest specifically, Quillhog anoints with the toxic bloom itself (extremely
  dangerous), while populations elsewhere use comparatively mild local irritants (a real hazard,
  but not remotely as severe). Extra caution is warranted specifically in Wet Forest; elsewhere it
  reads as a spiny nuisance, not a real threat.

- **Snaketail** — Temperate-Forest/Grassland ecotone resident, deliberately not deep-forest
  interior. Real base convergent on the tree hyrax (*Dendrohyrax*) — genuinely the closest real
  analog to "a rabbit that climbs trees," despite being unrelated to lagomorphs (a real,
  well-documented case of convergent evolution; tree hyraxes are more closely related to
  elephants than to any rabbit). Climbing uses the hyrax's real mechanism — moist, rubbery,
  high-friction foot pads rather than claw-hook gripping. Kept recognizably rabbit-like (long
  ears, general face/body character) rather than converging fully into a hyrax reskin: ears fold
  down by default to avoid catching on branches while climbing, raised only when alert, preserving
  the real directional-hearing advantage without paying the cost constantly. Tail is patterned to
  mimic Clicksnake's aposematic red-eye/black-yellow coloring — real Batesian mimicry, deliberately
  habitat-constrained to actually function: mimicry only works if local predators have real
  learned exposure to the model (real citation: scarlet kingsnake/coral snake mimicry
  effectiveness measurably drops outside the coral snake's actual range), so Snaketail is placed
  specifically at the Temperate-Forest/Grassland edge, where the same wide-ranging mesopredators
  that hunt in Clicksnake's Grassland territory plausibly also hunt here.

## 13. Mesopredators

Three species (expanded from the originally scoped two), deliberately diverse rather than one
dominant hunter — a direct consequence of mesopredator release: with the true apex predator
restricted to the alpine/tundra band, mid-tier hunters expand and diversify
across the rest of the island's range far more than they would under a dominant apex predator.

- **Treefox** — red-fox-anchored generalist, ranging Grassland, Woodland-Shrubland, and forest
  edges alike (real citation: red foxes are among the most widely distributed carnivores on Earth
  precisely because of this flexibility). Silver fox coloring — a real, naturally occurring red fox
  color morph, not an invention. Raccoon-style dexterous, hand-like forepaws (real citation:
  raccoon paw sensory processing rivals primate hand sensitivity) serve as both a visual marker and
  the physical seat of a real elephant-derived ground-vibration sense (real citation: elephants
  detect faint ground vibrations through Pacinian-corpuscle-rich foot cushions), letting it detect
  hidden prey before it can be seen. Fixed, non-retractable raccoon-style claws do double duty for
  climbing grip and prey-holding, wearing down ordinarily over time rather than staying
  artificially sharp. Hind ankles rotate nearly 180° (real squirrel/raccoon citation), enabling
  headfirst tree descent — invisible in normal standing or running posture, a genuine reveal only
  visible mid-climb. This costs some running speed and power (a real ambush-vs-pursuit predator
  trade-off), shifting its hunting strategy toward ambush and arboreal versatility, a plausible
  extension of real foxes' existing pounce-hunting behavior. Genuine paw dexterity extends to
  manipulating latches and containers, occasionally making it a nuisance around Círculo
  settlements — the same reputation real raccoons hold near human habitation. Its climbing ability
  makes it a real threat to Snaketail in the canopy, and because its range already includes
  Grassland, it's a second plausible vector (alongside Furypack) for carrying real, learned
  avoidance of Clicksnake into encounters with Snaketail — reinforcing why that species' tail
  mimicry actually functions rather than just being asserted.

- **Furypack** — pine-marten-anchored (~2-4 kg), ranging the Temperate-Forest/Grassland ecotone.
  The one deliberate change from its real base: mustelids are almost universally solitary hunters,
  but this species hunts in coordinated packs — a real behavioral toolkit borrowed from wolves and
  African wild dogs, essentially absent from real Mustelidae. Coordinated groups can take down
  prey well beyond what a lone marten could manage, including a real shot at overwhelming
  Quillhog's spines through sheer numbers. Alongside Treefox, its Grassland-adjacent range makes it
  a plausible vector for real, learned Clicksnake avoidance reaching Snaketail's habitat.

- **Twinshadows** — fossa-anchored, scaled up substantially to ~50-70 kg from the real fossa's
  ~5.5-8.6 kg. Real citation: the fossa (*Cryptoprocta ferox*) is a textbook case of convergent
  evolution — a Eupleridae relative of mongooses and civets that independently evolved a
  strikingly cougar-like ambush-predator body plan despite being unrelated to true cats, which is
  exactly the "different lineage evolved to hunt like a cougar" concept this species is built
  around. Melanistic dark dorsal coloring fading to a paler belly — real countershading (Thayer's
  law), stacking with the real citation that melanism is more common in dense, low-light forest
  habitats for camouflage. Silver eyes (reduced iris pigmentation combined with a strong tapetum
  lucidum) support low-light hunting. Hunts in bonded pairs of exactly two — real citation: coyotes
  cooperatively hunting in mated pairs against prey too large for one alone — a deliberately
  distinct mechanism from Furypack's larger coordinated pack, so the niche's two social hunters
  don't read as the same trick twice. Fills the "genuine threat to humans" role without stepping on
  the still-unbuilt alpine apex predator's territory: real cougars function as mesopredators, not
  apex hunters, in real ecosystems where wolves or bears are also present.

## 14. Birds

Three species: two island residents plus the previously-deferred sixth coastal migrant, finally
developed here alongside the rest of the niche. Residents follow the standing recombination method
(a real trait from a different Earth lineage, grafted into a combination Earth never produced); the
migrant stays closer to a real Earth template, consistent with how every other migratory species in
this document is handled.

**Native residents**

- **Trinketbird**, informally **Wingedthief** — bowerbird-anchored object-collector, ~25-35 cm,
  standard dexterous perching-bird feet. Plumage is deliberately plain gray-brown: a real bowerbird
  citation (the "extended phenotype") observes that many real bowerbird species stay cryptically
  colored precisely because their display lives externally, in the bower, rather than on the body —
  the same logic applies here, since Trinketbird's collecting behavior is itself the display. Bill is
  a robust, slightly hooked corvid-style tool built for gripping and working loose small hard
  objects, not just soft trinkets. One cross-lineage recombination: real magnetoreception (elsewhere
  in nature used for long-distance migratory navigation) repurposed instead for precise long-term
  memory of bower-site locations. A genuine practical nuisance around Círculo settlements — beyond
  decorative objects, it targets small Vértice crystals and loose wire or metal fittings, giving it
  the "Wingedthief" folk name and a real infrastructure-maintenance cost, not just a quirky habit.

- **Rustowl** — small, deliberately non-intimidating pygmy-owl-anchored hunter (real citation:
  *Glaucidium* pygmy owls, genuinely small-bodied, ~15-17 cm, with documented ferocity disproportionate
  to their size — sometimes taking prey larger than themselves). Reddish-bronze/rufous plumage, a
  real *Glaucidium* color-morph citation (gray and rufous morphs both occur in real pygmy owls; rufous
  was chosen here), and the source of the species' true documented name. Baseline biology is accurate
  real owl anatomy, kept unmodified: asymmetric ear placement for sound triangulation, and serrated
  flight feathers for near-silent flight. Real false eye-markings on the back of the head (a genuine
  trait in some real pygmy owl species, believed to deter mobbing) give rise to the folk nickname
  "two-headed bird." Two cross-lineage recombinations stack on this base: real pit-viper infrared
  heat-sensing, grafted onto the owl's facial disc (a reptile-to-bird trait crossing, the only one in
  this project running in that direction rather than out of a bird); and a real territorial-songbird
  behavior (the Beau Geste hypothesis, Krebs 1977 — real songbirds vary their song and move between
  perches to make one individual sound like a whole population, deceiving rivals about density),
  repurposed here as a hunting tactic: a lone Rustowl varies its calls and changes perches to sound
  like a chorus of many, intimidating prey into freezing (playing to the owl's heat sense) or bolting
  from cover (playing to its silent flight). Ordinary people correctly attribute the resulting sound
  to owls — it's known locally as the "owl choir" — but the fact that it's usually a single bird
  is more specialized, naturalist-level knowledge, consistent with this project's recurring pattern
  of dangerous or unusual fauna being known to most people only through indirect signs (the
  sky-watcher's feathers, Moonfur's territorial shift, Clicksnake's incidental clicking).

**Migratory visitor**

- **SummerVisitant**, commonly **Wetducks** — steamer-duck-anchored (real citation: *Tachyeres*, a
  real genus with flightless or weak-flying species that compensate with exceptional swimming and
  diving ability). Present seasonally on the island's interior lakes, opposite the real Visitantes'
  winter-solstice timing — hence the name. Flight structure is weak, built for short bursts rather
  than sustained travel, which is exactly what fuels genuine in-world biologist puzzlement: nobody
  has confirmed where they arrive from or depart to, since their build doesn't obviously support an
  ocean crossing. No confirmed explanation is documented publicly. Within that seasonal lake
  presence, the species keeps mainly to the lakes on the wooded western plateaus, rather than being
  spread evenly across every interior lake on the island.

- **Skydrifter** — see the migratory-visitor table above for the species itself. Its transit path,
  as far as anyone's tracked it, runs in a wide, fairly straight band over the North plains: an
  overflight this brief has no reason to detour around anything, so the crossing doesn't follow the
  terrain the way a resident or ground-bound species' range would. The exact line drifts somewhat
  toward the northeast or southwest from one passage to the next, not a fixed corridor.

## 15. Alpine prey base

Two species inhabiting the alpine/tundra band, together forming the prey base for the still-
undesigned apex predator (see §4 of `07_tappa7_regional_scenario.md`). Both pair a real alpine
mammal template with a trait recombination or extension, and this niche introduces a deliberate
functional pairing: two genuinely different predator-detection strategies working together rather
than one species duplicating the other.

- **Cryburrow** — groundhog-anchored colonial burrower (real base: *Marmota monax*, ~2-6.5 kg,
  ~40-65 cm), scaled up to roughly 1.3x a real groundhog's size. A real, if extrapolated,
  biomechanical trade-off follows directly from that: a larger body needs proportionally more soil
  excavated and wider tunnels dug to fit, slowing burrow-completion time relative to a
  standard-sized digger and forcing more time above ground than the species would otherwise
  prefer. Grizzled pale-gray/white fur is an extension of an already-real trait rather than a
  cross-lineage graft — the real hoary marmot (*Marmota caligata*) genuinely displays grizzled
  white-tipped fur — developed further here specifically as camouflage against rock, scree, and
  snow during that forced above-ground exposure. Its alarm system is a real cross-family
  recombination: prairie dogs' documented referential alarm calls (real research, Slobodchikoff et
  al. — calls that genuinely encode a predator's type, size, and behavior, not just a generic
  "danger" signal) grafted onto the marmot's own real sentinel/whistle behavior, which is far less
  differentiated in reality. Colonies can warn specifically enough that people living nearby can
  learn to read the calls.

- **Deergoat** — alpine-ibex-anchored (real *Capra ibex*: males ~65-100 kg, ~85-100 cm at the
  shoulder, curved ridged horns; females ~40 kg), kept at real scale and standard grayish-brown/tan
  coloring — deliberately undistorted from the real reference, since no functional pressure in this
  design calls for a size or color change, and staying visually distinct from Cryburrow's
  exaggerated pale coat keeps the two species reading as separate animals rather than palette-
  matched reskins of the same "alpine gray" look. One cross-species trait extension: ears scaled to
  real mule-deer proportions (already a genuine large-eared outlier among real ungulates), deeply
  cupped and funnel-shaped for enhanced sound capture, symmetric and highly mobile, able to pin
  flat back against the head in a real horse-style alert/flight response. Functions as a long-range
  passive acoustic detection system, complementing Cryburrow's near-range referential calls:
  together the two species give whatever hunts them — including the still-undesigned apex
  predator — two genuinely different warning channels to defeat, one social/acoustic-specific and
  one solitary/acoustic-directional.

This pairing deliberately constrains the eventual apex-predator design: a purely stealth/ambush
hunter would need to contend with both an early-warning colony network and individually excellent
hearing in its prey — a hook worth carrying forward when that final niche is designed, not a
problem to design around.

## 16. Alpine/tundra apex predator — Nacre

The final species in the fauna build-out, and the original open item from the dangerous-creatures
seed list (see `07_tappa7_regional_scenario.md` §4). Deliberately named apart from the rest of this
document's fauna: every other resident species uses a descriptive trait-compound name (Cryburrow,
Deergoat, Clicksnake, and so on); Nacre instead takes a single proper-noun name, the same register
already used for this world's other entities that sit apart from ordinary wildlife — Povo
Silencioso, the Visitantes. Unlike those names, Nacre's is chosen to evoke its own beauty rather
than a threat — a deliberate contrast with how the species is actually experienced by the people
who live near it. Nacre is still explicitly ordinary wildlife, not a Visitante or anything
Vértice-tied — the naming choice marks it as the capstone of this arc, not a change in category.

**Design method, distinct from every other resident species**: rather than a real modern-Earth
trait recombined into a new-to-Earth combination, Nacre is anchored directly on a real but extinct
Earth lineage — large dromaeosaurid theropod dinosaurs, most directly *Utahraptor*. This keeps the
same underlying discipline (a real, citable animal, not an invented one) while delivering a
scale and register nothing else in this document's fauna reaches.

**Base biology.** Roughly 7 m in body length and ~500 kg, matching real *Utahraptor* estimates;
hip height ~1.5-2 m, with an alert, head-raised standing height in the 2-3 m range. Feathered and
insulated, with a downy underlayer beneath the visible outer feathers handling cold tolerance
separately from visible coloring — grounded in real fossil evidence of feathers in close
dromaeosaurid relatives (quill knobs preserved on *Velociraptor* forearms) and in current
paleontological support for elevated, bird-like metabolic rates across maniraptoran theropods
generally. Metabolically, Nacre is modeled as mesothermic — a real, influential but still debated
hypothesis (Grady et al. 2014) proposing many dinosaurs sat metabolically between a cold-blooded
reptile and a fully endothermic mammal, needing meaningfully less food per kilogram of body mass
than a true endotherm of the same size. That lower relative energy demand, combined with ordinary
large-predator gorge-and-rest feeding behavior (real lions and tigers can rest for days digesting
a single large kill), is what makes Nacre's low hunting frequency plausible without inventing a
special ability.

**Coloring.** Countershaded — dark, cryptic gold-brown and tawny mottling across the back, pale
underside — tuned specifically to the Grassland and foothill terrain where its occasional larger
prey actually lives, not to its alpine home range, since its usual native prey (Cryburrow, Deergoat)
hunts by sound rather than sight and gains nothing from a predator camouflaged for vision. Real
precedent for reconstructing a feathered dinosaur's camouflage against its actual habitat's light
environment exists in the literature (Vinther et al. 2016, *Psittacosaurus*, via preserved
melanosome analysis). A structurally opalescent blue-white covers the upper body and crest, far
more extensive than a simple display patch — colors real mammals cannot produce (mammalian
coloration runs on melanin alone, capped at black/brown/red/yellow; true blue and white iridescence
in nature are almost always structural, a light-scattering keratin nanostructure effect, common
across birds and reptiles). Not concealment against its native alpine prey (Cryburrow/Deergoat
hunt by sound, so visual camouflage buys nothing there) — its real function is a pack-hunting tool:
Nacre hunts sighted, larger prey (Grassmothers-scale game, and now humans) in coordinated ambushes,
and the vivid coloring is used to intimidate and flush a target directly toward the rest of the
waiting pack. Displayed openly during upright posture and active hunts, not while stalking low to
the ground.

**Territory and population.** Confined to the alpine/tundra band not by natural preference but by
real anthropogenic range contraction — the same well-documented pattern behind real large
carnivores (snow leopards, tigers, historically European wolves) surviving only in the marginal
terrain human expansion hasn't claimed. Many generations of Círculo growth across the Grassland
lowlands pushed Nacre into the least hospitable remaining territory, not the territory it would
choose given open access to richer lowland prey. A real irony once there, not the reason for going
there: the same opalescent coloring that intimidates sighted prey on the Grassland also functions
as effective camouflage against snow and ice — incidental to the retreat, not its cause — so Nacre,
already the more serious threat lower down, becomes harder to see coming and more dangerous still
once genuinely in the alpine/Permanent Snow & Ice band, this time specifically against the humans
who pushed it there rather than against its native alpine prey, which still hunts and is hunted by
sound, unaffected by any of this. Only four known family packs exist across the entire alpine/tundra
band, each with a semi-mapped territory informally tracked and named by nearby communities using
cardinal/relative direction (e.g. "the South family") — the same way real communities near large
carnivores often track and name specific known individuals or groups.

**Pack structure.** Each pack numbers 4-6: a breeding pair plus offspring of mixed maturity
(subadults and juveniles). Real fossil support, honestly hedged rather than treated as settled: a
real Utahraptor bonebed (the Utah "Utahraptor Ridge"/Stikes Quarry site) preserved multiple
individuals, juveniles included, together — read by some paleontologists as evidence of gregarious
family-group behavior, contested by others as a predator-trap accumulation rather than proof of
coordinated hunting. Social structure otherwise follows real wolf-style dispersal: young males
leave their natal pack, range and hunt alone while gaining strength, then either challenge an
unrelated pack's established leader elsewhere or attach to an existing pack in a subordinate role
and pursue alternative reproductive strategies (real, documented behaviors in several social
carnivore and primate species). Leadership changes are violent but between unrelated males, not
patricide.

**Diet and incursion pattern.** Baseline diet is yaks — the domesticated livestock species most
exposed simply by sharing the alpine/Spine niche's geography (§7) — supplemented opportunistically
by wild alpine fauna, Cryburrow and Deergoat among them. This is chronic, low-level, and already
priced in by herders living in the range, not a crisis. Full descents into the Grassland lowlands
are rare and specifically seasonal, triggered by real, well-documented resource-scarcity-driven
prey-switching (the same mechanism behind real spikes in wolf and coyote livestock predation during
lean winters) — timed to late winter/early spring, around snowmelt, when accumulated snow has most
depleted both wild alpine prey and yak numbers. A descent is not a single-night raid: the pack
lingers near a kill for several days, feeding and defending the carcass before retreating back to
the peaks, making a sighting a sustained, escalating event rather than a single alert — the actual
"hurricane"-scale news event the rest of the population fears, in contrast to the constant,
manageable background risk yak herders already live with.

**Current narrative hook**: the southern pack recently underwent exactly this kind of leadership
change — an unrelated challenger, not a son, took over — and the new leader's inexperience is
driving more frequent-than-normal lowland incursions, breaking the established seasonal rarity
pattern. This reads as current, specific, trackable news ("the South family's been sighted at the
lower cliffs again") rather than a generic monster-attack alert, consistent with how every other
dangerous or unusual species in this document is known to ordinary people through specific,
indirect, trackable signs rather than abstract myth.

## 17. Decomposers and scavengers

A structural gap identified only after the main coast-to-tundra fauna arc (§8-§16) had already
closed: nothing built up to that point accounted for what happens to the biomass that arc's own
large-bodied residents produce — Nacre's multi-day kills, Grassmothers herd mortality, livestock
losses. Two species, deliberately wide-ranging rather than tied to a single biome, the same way
Quillhog isn't confined to one biome, since carrion turns up wherever something large enough dies.

- **Farsmell** — condor-scale vulture-anchored aerial scavenger, ~3 m wingspan (real Andean condor
  reference), soaring flight riding thermals for efficient wide-area search. Dark, somber plumage —
  a deliberately practical choice rather than a decorative one: carrion doesn't flee, so there's no
  camouflage pressure, and dark plumage carries a real thermoregulatory benefit, absorbing solar
  heat efficiently across long soaring searches. Bald head (real hygiene function, reducing
  bacterial buildup from feeding inside carcasses) and extremely corrosive stomach acid (real
  vultures run near pH 1, killing anthrax, botulism, and most other pathogens raw carrion carries)
  round out the baseline real biology. One recombination, an extension rather than a graft: real
  turkey vultures are already an outlier among birds for genuine olfactory sensitivity; Farsmell
  pushes that further past any real vulture's actual range, letting it detect carrion scent seeping
  out from beneath forest canopy its eyes can't see through — the same "amplify an already-real
  trait further" method already used for Cryburrow's fur and Snaketail's mimicry.

- **Meatcleaner** — Tasmanian-devil-anchored ground scavenger, kept close to real devil scale and
  coloring (~65-80 cm, 6-12 kg, dark brown-black fur with a white chest/shoulder blaze), since
  nothing in this design argues for a size or color departure. Real base biology: a bite force
  disproportionately strong for its size (among the highest bite-force-to-body-mass ratios of any
  living mammal) and a genuine, documented habit of consuming an entire carcass — bone, fur, and
  all — leaving almost nothing behind, which gives it a real ecological disease-control function by
  minimizing the window other pathogens have to spread from rotting remains, complementing
  Farsmell's own pathogen-killing gut chemistry. Real devils already feed communally and loudly,
  vocalizing/screeching competitively at a carcass; Meatcleaner extends this into a deliberate
  intimidation display, used to drive off rival scavengers and small predators from a claimed kill.
  One recombination: real snake/reptile vomeronasal (Jacobson's organ) chemoreception, tongue-
  flicking to sample and track ground-level scent trails with a directional precision no real
  mammal nose achieves — a genuine cross-class graft, and a deliberately different detection
  mechanism from Farsmell's airborne search, splitting the niche cleanly between long-range aerial
  scent detection and ground-trail tracking once something is already down.

Together, Farsmell's airborne search and pathogen-neutralizing gut and Meatcleaner's ground-trail
tracking and total-carcass consumption close the ecological loop this arc's large, dangerous
residents — Nacre above all — would otherwise leave open.

## 18. Transportation

Solar-electric rail (intercity) and trams (large Círculos, urban scale); electric utility
vehicles and bikes; riding animals (see above); and kite buggies/landboarding — a real technology,
well suited to this world's dominant Grassland biome and to lakes/coastline, with routes running
much better in one wind direction than the other given how consistently the local wind blows from
one bearing. Notably absent: no aircraft of any kind (balloons, ultralight, or otherwise) —
explained in-world by longstanding, well-founded fear of something dangerous in the upper sky that
reacts to anything with a wing-like silhouette. A few isolated, far-flung Círculos are rumored to
experiment with light aircraft anyway, far from where that danger is usually encountered. See §19,
Legends and Rumors, for the folklore this fear has produced.

Sea travel is deliberately limited rather than freely navigable: boats (which may be motorized)
run only on a small number of explicitly designated, scheduled corridors — most notably the ferry
connecting the mainland to the SW volcanic island — not across the open sea generally, which is
considered dangerous. Motorization shortens the trip but doesn't extend a boat's range beyond its
corridor.

Road and rail networks are also not uniformly cheap to build: a Wet Forest crossing costs more to
construct and maintain than an equivalent Grassland stretch, which should read in-world as sparser
infrastructure and slower travel through Povo Livre's home biome compared to the Círculo-dominated
Grassland interior.

## 19. Legends and Rumors

A dedicated home for this world's unconfirmed, secondhand folklore, consolidated from where it used
to sit scattered across other sections. Deliberately kept apart from the rest of this document's
fauna and society entries, which describe things that are (as far as this document goes) real and
confirmed: everything below is explicitly rumor, partial sighting, and folk interpretation, not
settled fact — in-world, most people who repeat these tales have never had a direct encounter
themselves, and nobody has connected any of them to each other or to a known species.

- **The sky-watcher** (also "the cloud hunter") — the reason this world has no aircraft. Pilots and
  would-be flight-attempters tell of something that hides in the cloud layer and destroys any
  aircraft that gets too high; the only physical evidence anyone has ever recovered is a handful of
  strikingly beautiful, pale, iridescent feathers, fueling a folk belief that it's some enormous
  bird. A few isolated, far-flung Círculos are rumored to experiment with light aircraft anyway, far
  from where the danger is usually encountered.

- **The giant serpent** — isolated mountain communities near the northern peaks tell of an
  enormous, pale serpent glimpsed rarely on the high snow line. No attacks are attributed to it,
  only sightings, and it's treated as a distinct, unrelated creature from the sky-watcher of the
  pilots' stories. Some tellers link the sightings to Nacre — a folk theory, never confirmed on
  either side, and not something Nacre's own known behavior obviously explains (Nacre is
  quadrupedal, feathered, and firmly earthbound, not serpentine).

- **The lands overseas** — sea travel runs only on a small number of designated, scheduled
  corridors, never across the open sea generally, which is treated as genuinely dangerous. Old
  sailors and fishing crews occasionally tell of coastlines or lights glimpsed far past the known
  ferry route, further out than any Círculo vessel has confirmed reaching. Never substantiated —
  dismissed by most as fog and fatigue — but persistent enough that a few old charts still mark the
  edge of the known sea with a warning rather than leaving it simply blank.

- **The hunter of the deep seas** — grounded in an existing, previously unattached fishermen's
  rumor (§8): Shadowless Sardine schools occasionally scatter without visible cause, something never
  actually sighted, only inferred from the school's reaction. The same folk name gets reached for
  whenever an expected Green Mackerel bloom fails to arrive, or a boat is lost on one of the rarer
  long-corridor crossings, beyond what Sea Terror's own known, irregular arrivals already account
  for. Whether it names a real, separate phenomenon or is simply what people call the
  unpredictability already built into Sea Terror's documented behavior is left open.

- **The guardian of Povo Silencioso** — genuine encounters with Povo Silencioso are rare, and most
  of what circulates about them is secondhand. Some attribute that near-total absence of confirmed
  contact to something beyond ordinary isolation — a guardian, described inconsistently from telling
  to telling, said to keep outsiders from getting close to the NE archipelago at all.

Room for further entries as other lore develops (an eventual increment to the Povo Silencioso
material is the likeliest next addition).

## 21. Vértices — the magic system

Public framing only: how Vértice practice actually works, mechanically and materially. Where this
knowledge originally came from is genuinely unsettled even in-world, and is deliberately left out
of this document.

**The model.** A Vértice crystal works like a crystal radio receiver: the crystal is the hardware,
gated by a real physical property of the specific mineral (piezoelectric, birefringent, zeolitic,
magnetic, or — for Mente only — a paired-conductor arrangement rather than a single mineral). Which
*domain* (noun) a person can reach is fixed by which crystal they hold. Which *verb* they can
perform within that domain is gated separately, by the practitioner's own skill — "filtering the
harmonics," not a property of the crystal at all.

**Domains, materials, and where they're mined:**

| domain | Substantivos | crystal | source rock | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Matéria | Sólido / Líquido / Gasoso | laumontite (zeolite) | greywacke | veinlets/joints, real NZ citation |
| Onda | Som / Luz / Calor | quartz | schist | same orogenic veins as gold, birefringence |
| Bios | Fauna / Flora / Corpo | vivianite | sedimentary basin fill | forms from organic decay in floodplain sediment |
| Energia | Cinética / Potencial / Elétrica | mica (muscovite/biotite) | schist | rarer than the quartz from the same veins, piezoelectric |
| Campo | Gravidade / Magnetismo / Pressão | magnetite | volcanic (primary); reworked placer in sedimentary basin fill (weak, secondary) | real paleomagnetism citation |
| Mente | Pensamento / Emoção / Memória | copper (common) / silver (rare), refined with a black fungus native to Wet Forest | volcanic amygdules (copper), volcanic epithermal veins (silver) | the only domain requiring an assembled device, not a single mineral — refinement method still under study, only discovered a few years ago |
| Tempo | — | none | — | no crystal exists; nobody has ever accessed this domain |

Gold and jade/pounamu, both schist-associated, are valuable mundane materials, not Vértice
crystals — neither has a property that gates a domain.

**The verbs**, roughly ordered by how invasive the intervention is: Sentir/Localizar (perceive),
Emitir/Ativar (trigger something latent), Mover/Direcionar (relocate), Moldar/Formatar (reshape),
Modificar/Sobrecarregar (alter an intrinsic property), Conectar/Sincronizar (link two things to act
as one), and Legado/Root ("make it permanent") — which is not a verb anyone has ever managed on any
domain, for any purpose. Making a change stick without renewal appears to be categorically
different from making the change itself, and remains entirely theoretical.

**Reality pushback**: nothing can be created from nothing. Forcing unnatural change (e.g. extreme
accelerated growth) causes the result to be weaker or to fail outright; food or water produced this
way doesn't actually nourish.

**Social note**: Mente is the domain most Círculos are uneasy about, for two compounding reasons —
the ability itself (reaching into another person's thoughts or memories) is inherently invasive,
and its source material is tied to Povo Livre-controlled Wet Forest territory, making practitioners
dependent on land and knowledge outside Círculo control. Adoption of Vértice practice generally has
been uneven since it resurfaced in living memory — fastest among Círculos Progressistas.

**Verb order and mastery, per domain.** Each domain developed its own natural order of mastery,
driven by what that domain is actually used for, not a single universal ladder. E = established,
common trade knowledge. R = achieved, real, but rare. U = unclaimed — nobody has ever done it, a
genuine frontier:

| domain | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Matéria | Sentir (E) | Mover (E) | Modificar (E) | Moldar (E) | Emitir (R) | Conectar (R) |
| Onda | Sentir (E) | Emitir (E) | Mover (E) | Moldar (E) | Modificar (R) | Conectar (U) |
| Bios | Sentir (E) | Emitir (E) | Modificar (E) | Moldar (R) | Mover (R) | Conectar (U) |
| Energia | Sentir (E) | Emitir (E) | Mover (E) | Modificar (R) | Moldar (R) | Conectar (U) |
| Campo | Sentir (E) | Emitir (E) | Mover (R) | Moldar (R) | Modificar (U) | Conectar (U) |
| Mente | Sentir (E) | Mover (E) | Emitir (R) | Moldar (U) | Modificar (U) | Conectar (U) |

Matéria and Onda are the oldest, most practically load-bearing domains (construction/water;
communication/senses) and reach furthest. Mente reaches least far — newest in practical terms,
dependent on an assembled rather than mined crystal, and practiced under social suspicion.
Bios is constrained less by unfamiliarity than by its own reality-pushback friction: acting on a
living thing's own agency (Mover — physically overpowering a creature's body) is harder than
adjusting a property it already has (Modificar), which is why Bios's order departs from the
"expected" universal shape.

## 22. Industries, economy, and technology

Scenario-chat output (see `claude/index.md`'s recommendation to start a dedicated scenario thread),
building on top of Tappa 6's siting and Tappa 8's lithology. Treat everything in this section as
more provisional than the geography above — it postdates the current Vértice materials table (§21)
and will be revisited once Tappa 8's planned granite/limestone additions land.

**22.1 Economic base, no longer a single assumption.** Tappa 6 (`06_tappa6_suitability.md` §3)
locked "open-field agriculture" as the suitability model's economic assumption, and that's why
Grassland outscored every other biome. Rather than reopening site selection, economic-base
diversity is layered on **post-hoc**, the same way architecture style already is (06 doc §0): the
13 Grassland Círculos read as open-field-agriculture-based; the 2 Temperate Forest sites
(Circulo_C_25k, Circulo_E3_2k) and 2 Woodland/Shrubland sites read as forestry/craft-leaning
economies; Circulo_D (SW Island, volcanic) reads as geothermal/mineral-leaning. Círculo
coordinates from Tappa 6 stay authoritative and current-state; if a future pass narratively
relocates a Círculo, the new site supersedes the old one for all downstream Tappas (transportation
routing above all), and the abandoned original becomes a ruins point of interest rather than being
erased. Given the scale of moving an entire population, a full Círculo relocation is treated as a
rare, major event — a small (625-2,000 person) Círculo founded as an offshoot of a larger inland
one is the lower-cost, more demographically plausible way to add a coastal-leaning settlement
without touching the fixed 17.

**22.2 Fishing.** River/lake fishing is a safe universal baseline given how dense this world's
stream network already is (82.5% of land within 0.5 km of a stream, Tappa 6 §1) — every Círculo
plausibly has some access, no siting change needed. Coastal fishing is a much bigger ask
geographically: only one of the 17 sites sits under 1 km from the ocean, the rest 2-17 km inland
(06 doc §8). Rather than resiting, coastal fishing economies belong to dedicated small outposts —
the same pattern already established for alpine resource work along the Spine (§7 above) — not
full Círculos.

**22.3 Primary sector by lithology.** Tappa 8 v5's locked classes (`08_tappa8_geomorphology.md` §3):

| lithology class | area | Vértice yield (§21) | mundane/industrial yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| schist | 1,780.9 km² | gold-quartz veins (Onda), muscovite/biotite mica (Energia) | gold, jade/pounamu (both mundane-valuable, not Vértice) |
| greywacke | 3,098.5 km² | laumontite (Matéria) | general building/construction stone — open, see Tappa 8's planned granite/limestone additions |
| sedimentary basin fill | 4,437.3 km² | vivianite (Bios), weak reworked magnetite (Campo) | clay/lime (plaster, already locked in Tappa 8), likely the primary agricultural substrate — spatial overlap with Grassland biome is plausible but **not yet verified against the rasters**, treat as hypothesis |
| volcanic | 595.7 km² | magnetite (Campo), native silver (Mente) | geothermal potential (3 known vent sites feed the lava-tube model) |

Worth flagging as a real, deliberate tension rather than a gap: volcanic is the scarcest class (6%
of classified land) and the sole source of both Campo's magnetite and Mente's copper/silver — a
natural resource-competition point between Vértice practice and any conventional metallurgy that
also wants magnetite as an iron-ore precursor.

**22.4 Bio-engineered materials — the Bios-Vértice materials industry.** Mechanism: Bios's
Modificar verb is already established, common trade knowledge (E-tier, §21's mastery table) —
"altering an intrinsic property" of a living organism is routine, not exceptional. Rather than
escalating to a rarer verb tier for more dramatic material outcomes, the degree of change scales
the **labor and reality-pushback cost within the same tier**: minor hardiness/yield tweaks are
light, sustainable work; inducing a genuinely novel functional property (e.g., electrical
conductivity) is the same verb and tier, but a much heavier, more fragile daily/weekly maintenance
burden. This is not a stylistic choice — it's necessitated by Legado/Root being entirely unclaimed
on every domain (§21): nothing in this system can be made permanent, so a Bios-modified cultivar
can only be *kept* that way through continuous work. A Bios materials practitioner reads as a
farmer/agronomist, not a wizard — this industry is a specialized agricultural profession
(plausibly under a Médios-tier "agriculture" council, §2), not a factory.

Two species, deliberately kept minimal:
- **Insulator**: a cool-climate latex-bearing cultivar, real-world grounded in *Taraxacum
  kok-saghyz* (Russian/Kazakh dandelion, a genuine temperate/steppe rubber-latex source,
  historically grown for rubber in place of tropical Hevea) — fits the Lowland Steppe/Grassland
  biome (the Central Otago/Mackenzie Basin analog already established in
  `biome_landscape_characteristics.md`). Natural latex/rubber as an electrical insulator has a
  century of real precedent (vulcanized rubber, gutta-percha cable insulation).
- **Conductor**: a bio-conductive material grounded in real cable bacteria — filamentous bacteria
  genuinely documented conducting electricity over centimeter-scale distances through anoxic
  sediment. Placed in the same basin-fill floodplain/wetland environment where vivianite already
  forms (§21: "forms from organic decay in floodplain sediment"), giving the industry's two source
  materials a shared, lore-consistent home rather than scattered geography.

Both are genuinely new and small-scale (see §22.5 — this industry is only as old as Vértice
itself), not yet load-bearing infrastructure; conventional copper wiring is assumed to still carry
the bulk of the grid.

**Explicitly not extended to animals**: Círculo councils would strongly oppose Bios-Modificar
applied to any sentient being — animal ecotype divergence (yak, reindeer, sheep, dogs, chickens,
§7) stays entirely mundane-selective-breeding, unrelated to this mechanism. Open question, not yet
decided: whether this taboo is understood to cover only clearly sapient peoples, or animals
generally.

**22.5 Two-track technology model.** The setting's tech base splits cleanly into two tracks of very
different age and distribution, rather than one flat tech tree:

| | Track 1 — the old base | Track 2 — the new layer |
|---|---|---|
| age | centuries-old, matured | ~20-30 years, human-only (matches §21's Vértice age) |
| distribution | universal, all 17 Círculos, politically neutral | uneven — faster among Círculos Progressistas, slower/resisted among Conservadores and de Isolamento (§21) |
| includes | rail/trams/electric vehicles/kite buggies (§18), solar PV, sodium-ion batteries, geothermal (Caldária), conventional/empirical medicine, telegraph (strung along existing rail corridors, inheriting the same Wet Forest cost penalty as the rail itself), amateur/hobbyist radio (vacuum-tube era, fills the gaps telegraph's fixed wires can't reach — remote outposts, travelers, off-rail small Círculos), typewriters (universal, personal-scale), efficient printing (Médios/Grandes-concentrated; smaller Círculos route manuscripts to the nearest Arquigrado), Hollerith-style electromechanical punch-card tabulation for stock/trade data (Médios/Grandes), paper ledgers/card catalogs (universal) | Bios-Modificar bio-materials (§22.4), Bios-Corpo medicine as an emerging complement/rival to conventional medicine, Onda-based long-range communication/perception — person-bound, requiring no built infrastructure at all, which is what it offers that telegraph/radio structurally can't |

Computing is absent on both tracks — not part of what centuries of indirect,
prompt-toward-self-discovery guidance ever nudged toward, and nothing in Vértice's own domain set
naturally substitutes for general-purpose computation the way Onda substitutes for communication or
Bios-Corpo substitutes for medicine.

Settlement tier (Grande/Médio/Pequeno, §2) tracks Track 1 capital intensity. Progressista/
Conservador/de Isolamento alignment (named in §21 but never yet mapped onto the 17 actual sites —
open item) tracks Track 2 adoption, and the two axes are meant to be **partially independent**: a
small Progressista Círculo can be materially modest but genuinely advanced on Track 2, while a
large, wealthy Conservador Círculo stays technologically stagnant there despite its size.

**22.6 Craft, philosophy, and the arts.** Consistent with §1's "no rigid hierarchies...
encouragement toward versatility, study, and individual expression": labor carries no inherent
negative connotation, and a Círculo with its necessities covered by Track 1 infrastructure plus a
modest Track 2 layer channels a meaningful share of its effort into personalized craft —
clothing, textile work, carved doors and furniture — rather than large-scale standardized
production. Dominant decorative idiom: **Art Nouveau** (organic, plant/vine-derived curvilinear
form), which fits a Bios-literate, nature-integrated culture with little justification needed.
**Art Deco** appears narrowly rather than as a competing universal style — its geometric
sunburst/ray motifs read specifically as solar-energy iconography, reserved for civic or
energy-infrastructure contexts (a Grande Círculo's solar array housing, a rail station) rather than
everyday craft goods. This decorative language sits on top of the climate-driven architecture
styles already locked in §4 (Terrapedra/Trançada/Salina/Caldária/Modular) rather than replacing
them — structural typology and ornamental idiom are separate layers.

Worth naming explicitly so it doesn't read as an accidental gap: Track 1 infrastructure (rail,
solar, batteries) still needs disciplined, standardized, unglamorous expert labor to keep running.
That work carries its own form of respect — expertise and reliability — distinct from the
personal-identity and status weight carried by decorative craft; the two aren't in competition, but
the setting should be deliberate that infrastructure work isn't devalued just because it isn't
where the "craftsman style" applies its energy.
